Academic Project · Purdue University

VetGPT: Pre-Training a GPT Model from Scratch

Built a domain-specific GPT language model entirely from scratch — without starting from a pretrained checkpoint — to explore transformer architecture and training dynamics firsthand, then applied it to veterinary diagnostic reasoning.

  • Pre-trained a GPT-style transformer from scratch using PyTorch and CUDA, distributing training across 4 GPUs to handle the compute load of full pre-training.
  • Curated a training corpus of 50,000+ records using custom-built web crawlers to source veterinary and diagnostic text data.
  • Cleaned, tokenized, and structured the dataset for language model pre-training, iterating on architecture and hyperparameters to improve convergence.
  • Achieved 82% accuracy on veterinary diagnostic classification tasks, validating the model's ability to reason over domain-specific medical language.
